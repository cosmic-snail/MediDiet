- Excerpt window 2: chronic kidney disease. ## Determining fluid volumes to remove by dialysis One of the main aims of dialysis is to remove fluid which builds up because of reduced renal function. Determining the volume of fluid to be removed by dialysis involves identifying a target weight for a person. This is often defined as how much a person should weigh at the end of a haemodialysis session or, for people who have peritoneal dialysis, how much they should weigh in the morning. Comparing a person's current and target weight helps to decide the amount of fluid to be removed during dialysis. Assessing fluid status to set, or adjust, a person's target dialysis weight is usually based on clinical judgement and identifying symptoms of over or underhydration.
