# WHO Sodium Intake Guideline Source Card

## Source Notes

Short research source card derived from a public guideline record. The source card stores only summary and short excerpt content.

## Copyright Handling

Do not store full copyrighted source documents in this repository.

## Extractable Source Content

Adults should reduce sodium intake to less than 2 g/day. This is equivalent to less than 2000 mg sodium per day and is relevant to hypertension and cardiovascular disease prevention.
