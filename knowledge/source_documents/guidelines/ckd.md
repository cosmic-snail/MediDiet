# Chronic Kidney Disease (CKD) Dietary Guidelines

Source: KDIGO 2024 Clinical Practice Guideline

## Sodium Restriction

For patients with CKD stages 1-5, dietary sodium should be limited to less than
2000 mg per day. Per meal, sodium intake should not exceed 700 mg to avoid
excessive postprandial sodium load. High-sodium foods include processed meats,
canned soups, pickled vegetables, and salty snacks. Patients should be advised
to avoid adding salt at the table and to choose low-sodium alternatives.

## Potassium Management

Serum potassium should be monitored regularly. For patients with hyperkalemia
(serum K+ > 5.0 mmol/L), dietary potassium intake should be restricted to
2000-3000 mg per day. High-potassium foods to limit or avoid: bananas, oranges,
potatoes, tomatoes, avocados, and dried fruits.

## Phosphorus Control

Dietary phosphorus should be limited to 800-1000 mg per day for patients with
CKD stages 3-5. High-phosphorus foods to avoid include: dairy products
(cheese, milk, yogurt), nuts and seeds, dark colas, processed foods with
phosphate additives, and organ meats.

## Protein Intake

For non-dialysis CKD patients (stages 3-5), dietary protein should be limited
to 0.55-0.60 g/kg body weight per day. For patients on dialysis, protein
requirements increase to 1.0-1.2 g/kg/day. Plant-based protein sources are
preferred over animal proteins to reduce acid load and phosphorus absorption.

## Fluid Management

Fluid intake may need to be restricted based on urine output and presence of
edema. Typical restriction: 500-750 mL plus daily urine output volume.
